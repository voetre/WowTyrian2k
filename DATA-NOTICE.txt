WowTyrian2k: game data notice
=============================

The "full" release of WowTyrian2k includes Tyrian's graphics, sound, music and
game data, converted for the WoW client:

  Textures/   sprites, tiles and pictures, re-packed as TGA atlases
  Sound/      sound effects, and the music rendered from music.mus to OGG
  Data/       levels, enemies, items and text, converted to Lua tables;
              levgen.lua holds segments cut from the original maps

This data is NOT covered by the GNU GPL that applies to the WowTyrian2k code.
All rights remain with its authors and rights holders. It is included for free
use only and must not be sold.


Where it comes from
-------------------

Tyrian (c) 1995 Epic MegaGames Inc and Eclipse Software.

Tyrian 2.1 was later released as freeware. The data was converted from the
freeware Tyrian 2.1 files, the same files the OpenTyrian project distributes:
  https://github.com/opentyrian/opentyrian
  https://camanis.net/tyrian/tyrian21.zip

In April 2007 Daniel Cook, Tyrian's level artist, released his remastered
Tyrian ship and tile graphics for anyone to use and modify ("use and abuse
them as desired"):
  https://lostgarden.com/2007/04/05/free-game-graphics-tyrian-ships-and-tiles/
